❤️ Our Story

Passion for baking since 2020

🌸 Rihana Meer's

Founded by Rihana Meersa, our bakery brings together traditional Ethiopian baking heritage and modern French pastry techniques. Every morning at 4 AM, our ovens fire up to create the freshest breads, pastries, and cakes in Addis Ababa.

What started as a small home bakery has grown into a beloved local institution, serving the community with love and dedication. We believe that good food brings people together, and every bite should tell a story of quality and care.

👩‍🍳 Meet the Baker

Rihana Meersa

Head Baker & Founder

Rihana's journey into baking began in her grandmother's kitchen, where she learned the secrets of traditional Ethiopian breads. After training at prestigious pastry schools in Paris and Vienna, she returned to Addis Ababa to create something special - a bakery that honors both local traditions and international techniques.

"Baking is my meditation. Every morning, the aroma of fresh bread reminds me why I love what I do." - Rihana
