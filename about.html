🌸 Our Journey

Founded by Rihana Megersa, our bakery brings together traditional Ethiopian baking heritage and modern French pastry techniques. Every morning at 4 AM, our ovens fire up to create the freshest breads, pastries, and cakes in Addis Ababa.

What started as a small home bakery has grown into a beloved local institution, serving the community with love and dedication. We believe that good food brings people together, and every bite should tell a story of quality and care.
