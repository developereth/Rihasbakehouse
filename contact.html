Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer delivery?

Yes! We deliver within Addis Ababa. Delivery is free for orders over ETB 1,500. Standard delivery fee is ETB 150.

How far in advance should I order?

For regular orders, 24 hours notice is appreciated. For custom cakes and large catering orders, please contact us at least 3-5 days in advance.

Do you offer gluten-free or vegan options?

Yes! We offer a selection of gluten-free breads and vegan pastries. Please mention dietary requirements when ordering.

Do you make wedding cakes?

Absolutely! We specialize in custom wedding cakes. Contact our catering team for a free consultation and tasting.
